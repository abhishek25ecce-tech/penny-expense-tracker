Penny – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Penny is a simple expense tracker designed to help you manage your daily expenses and understand your spending.

🔒 Your Data Stays Yours

  • We do not store your expense data on our own servers.
  • Your expenses remain in your own Google Sheet, in your Google Drive.
  • Your financial details belong to you. Penny helps you record, view, and manage them.
  • We do not sell your personal or financial data.
  • You can manage or delete your expenses directly in Google Sheets.

Google Account Access

Penny uses Google authorization to access your spreadsheet and provide expense-tracking features. You can revoke this access through your Google Account settings at any time.

Your Privacy, Your Control

Penny is built to help you manage your expenses while keeping your data under your control. Deleting the app or revoking access does not automatically delete your Google Sheet.
